Why Open Hearts Club

Open-heart surgery changes more than a heart. It affects patients, caregivers and families.

Open Hearts Club is being built around a simple observation: no one should have to face that journey feeling alone.

Our purpose is to connect lived experience with practical education, encouragement and community - before surgery, during recovery and in the life that follows.

Open Hearts Club

No one walks the journey alone.

Open Hearts Club provides peer support, community and educational information. It does not provide medical diagnosis or individualized medical advice. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals about medical care.
