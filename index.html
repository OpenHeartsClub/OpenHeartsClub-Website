Open-heart surgery is a journey no one travels alone.

No one walks the journey alone.

If you’re here because you or someone you love is either facing or has already gone through open-heart surgery, we’re glad you found us.

Open Hearts Club exists to provide inspiration, education, peer support and community for patients, survivors, caregivers and families before, during and after surgery.

Why Open Hearts ClubWalk with us

A complete journey: before surgery, through recovery, and into the life that follows.

There is a place for you here.

Facing Surgery

Find perspective, practical education and people who understand the uncertainty before an operation.

Survivors

Recovery does not end at discharge. Connect with others who understand the physical and emotional journey.

Caregivers & Families

The people beside the patient travel the journey too. Open Hearts Club is being built for them as well.

What we're building

Digital Campus

Educational resources and community spaces for the full open-heart-surgery journey.

Journey Chapters

Local connection through conversations, coffee, gatherings and shared experience.

Nutrition Center

Heart-conscious food should still taste good. Our Nutrition Center will focus on practical, enjoyable choices.
