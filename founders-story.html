Founder's Story

A murmur, open-heart surgery, and the people who were not always surrounded by visitors.

The idea for Open Hearts Club grew from an open-heart-surgery journey and from seeing how much the presence of family and community matters during hospitalization and recovery.

The organization is being built so patients, survivors and caregivers can find people walking the same road.

Open Hearts Club

No one walks the journey alone.

Open Hearts Club provides peer support, community and educational information. It does not provide medical diagnosis or individualized medical advice. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals about medical care.
